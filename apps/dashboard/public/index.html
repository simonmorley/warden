Warden

Phishing triage where the AI has to earn the right to act.

What this is

An AI can tell you a page looks like phishing in a second. Deciding whether it may take that page down by itself is the hard part. Warden makes it earn that permission from evidence, and take it away the moment it gets one wrong.

Nothing is being trained

The model and the prompt never change. There is no learning, no tuning, no feedback into the AI at all. Warden only ever measures a fixed classifier and decides what it is allowed to do.

The verdict never authorises itself

The AI returns a verdict and the evidence behind it. Separate arithmetic it cannot influence decides whether that becomes a block or goes to a person — so a page that fools the model still can't act.

Permission is earned, then lost

It starts able only to recommend. Enough confirmed-correct calls and it may block a URL on its own. One confirmed mistake on something it blocked and the permission is gone and the block is undone.

  1. Recommends onlyEverything goes to a person. The record builds.
  2. On trialThe evidence is strong enough, but it must hold for ten more calls.
  3. Acts aloneIt may block a URL itself — up to three awaiting review at once.
